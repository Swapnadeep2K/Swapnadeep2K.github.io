About

I started in electronics, ended up in data and personalization, and now spend most of my time getting AI tools to do useful work inside both.

I'm Swapnadeep Sarkar. I work as a Technical Consultant at Adobe, where I spend most of my time on enterprise MarTech and customer experience platforms — architecting the CDP-style customer data systems underneath, designing cross-channel orchestration, and running experimentation and personalization for marketing programs that span email, push, SMS, in-app and web.

My background is in electronics: a B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering from VIT gave me an early grounding in systems and signals. That systems-thinking is a big part of why I went back for an M.Tech in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning at BITS Pilani — completed part-time, alongside full-time work, with a 9.68 CGPA — and why most of my recent projects sit at the seam between data infrastructure and applied AI.

Day to day that means Python automation and REST API workflows on the backend, React Native and JavaScript on the frontend, and — increasingly — building AI-agent integrations that let LLMs act directly on marketing platforms: querying audiences, optimizing offers, generating experiment plans.
