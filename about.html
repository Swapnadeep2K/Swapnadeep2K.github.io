About

From engineering to MarTech and applied AI/ML, I build systems that connect customer data, journeys, experimentation and personalization.

I'm Swapnadeep Sarkar, a Technical Consultant at Adobe working across enterprise MarTech and customer experience platforms.

My work spans the full customer-experience lifecycle, from designing customer-data foundations and integrating behavioral and profile data to building audiences, orchestrating omnichannel journeys, measuring performance, and using experimentation, personalization, decisioning, and recommendations to improve digital customer experiences.

I hold a B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering from VIT, Vellore and an M.Tech in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning from BITS Pilani, which I pursued while working full-time. Over the years, my focus has evolved from a broader engineering foundation into customer experience technology, data, experimentation, personalization, and applied AI.

Day to day, I work across customer-data models and ingestion, identity and audience design, journey and campaign orchestration, analytics implementation and measurement, experimentation, personalization, recommendations, and offer and experience decisioning. My experience also includes Python automation, REST API workflows, and frontend integrations, used to solve implementation challenges and simplify how these systems are operated at scale.

More recently, I've been building AI-agent solutions that work directly with MarTech platforms. I use MCP integrations, prompt engineering, and reusable AI skills to query audiences and analytics, retrieve experimentation knowledge, plan tests, optimize offers, and simplify journey and campaign workflows.

Overall, my work sits at the intersection of MarTech engineering, customer data, experimentation, personalization, and applied AI, connecting these capabilities to build scalable and measurable customer experiences.
