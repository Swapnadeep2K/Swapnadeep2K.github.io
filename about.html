About

I started in electronics, moved into customer data and personalization, and now build systems that connect data, journeys, experimentation, analytics and applied AI/ML.

I'm Swapnadeep Sarkar, a Technical Consultant at Adobe working across enterprise MarTech and customer experience platforms.

My work covers the full lifecycle of a customer experience: architecting customer-data foundations, bringing together behavioral and profile data, building audiences, orchestrating journeys and campaigns, measuring performance, and using experimentation, personalization, decisioning and recommendations to improve what customers experience across channels.

My background is in electronics. A B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering from VIT gave me an early grounding in systems and signals. That systems perspective led me to pursue an M.Tech in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning at BITS Pilani while working full-time—and continues to shape how I approach data, decisioning and customer-experience problems.

Day to day, I move between customer-data models and pipelines, identity and audience design, omnichannel journey orchestration, analytics instrumentation and measurement, experimentation strategy, recommendations, offer and experience decisioning, and production troubleshooting. I also build Python automation, REST API workflows and frontend integrations that make these systems easier to operate at scale.

More recently, I build AI-agent solutions that work directly with MarTech platforms — using MCP integrations and reusable skills to query audiences and analytics, retrieve experiment knowledge, plan tests, optimize offers and simplify journey and campaign workflows.

What I do

Design scalable personalization solutions grounded in customer data—bringing together audiences, journeys, experimentation, recommendations, decisioning, analytics and measurement.

What I'm exploring
