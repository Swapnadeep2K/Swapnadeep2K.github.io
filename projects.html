MCP-connected marketing assistants

Technical Consultant, Adobe · 2025–Present

Problem: Marketers and consultants working across AJO, AEP, CJA and Target had no direct way to query or act on that data through conversational tools — every check meant switching into each platform's UI.

Contribution: Explored and built MCP integrations connecting those platforms to Claude, ChatGPT and Adobe Coworker, including an internal solution for email-size optimization and Experience Decisioning.

Outcome: Reduced email size by 35–40% when applied to migration projects, and made platform data queryable through natural-language tooling instead of manual UI navigation.

Experiment Accelerator — Adobe Coworker skills

Technical Consultant, Adobe · 2025

Problem: Marketers running A/B tests through Adobe Target had to piece together past experiment context and sample-size math manually before launching anything new.

Contribution: Designed and shipped two Adobe Coworker skills — a RAG-based Experiment Knowledge Base with vector search over past experiments, and a conversational sample-size calculator.

Outcome: Simplified experiment discovery and planning, cutting the setup work needed before a test could go live.

Customer data pipelines on Adobe Experience Platform

Technical Consultant, Adobe · 2022–Present

Problem: Marketing teams needed a unified, real-time view of customers assembled from multiple upstream systems, with identity spread across sources.

Contribution: Architected XDM schemas (Profile/Event/Lookup), built batch and streaming ingestion, and wrote SQL transformations in Data Distiller for identity stitching, profile unification and audience segmentation.

Outcome: Enabled omnichannel activation and real-time profile enrichment feeding directly into AJO and Target campaigns.
